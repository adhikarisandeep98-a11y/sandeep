Our Story
More Than a
Supermarket

Klein's is a bit different than your everyday corporate supermarket. We are a family-owned store and have been proudly serving the Fairmount section of Philadelphia for over 130 years.

Instead of worrying about things like margins and marketing, we concentrate on keeping our customers happy — and we think it shows. We carry quality items at reasonable prices.
